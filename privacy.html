Tel-Jay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tel-Jay is a set of audio effects for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is made to work entirely on your device.

What Tel-Jay collects

Nothing. Tel-Jay does not collect, store or send personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

Microphone

If you choose the microphone or an audio input as the source, Tel-Jay plays the sound through the effect as you play. The sound is processed on your device and is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Tel-Jay remembers a few settings so it opens as you left it: the last effect you used, the speaker cabinet you chose, your audio input, and any presets you save. They stay on your device, and deleting the app removes them. When you use the effects in another app, such as GarageBand, that app saves their settings with your project.

On a Mac

If you choose to, Tel-Jay can read Apple Loops and Apple's speaker recordings that are already on your Mac to play them through an effect. It only reads them; it does not change or send them.

TestFlight and the App Store

If you test Tel-Jay through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. Apple's own privacy policy covers what Apple collects.

Children

Tel-Jay collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
